# The YM2612's DAC channel reaches the sample export.
#
# This chip is the odd one out: the others name a range of a ROM with registers, while the YM2612 is
# handed one byte at a time at 0x2A and the driver decides where those bytes came from. `02 Green
# Hill Zone` carries them in a `0x67` block of type 0x00 - 13 132 bytes - streamed into the DAC with
# `0x80-0x8F` and sought into with `0xE0`.
#
# **The unit is the hit the driver actually played**, not the region between two `0xE0` offsets: the
# offsets are seek targets and the driver routinely plays straight through one into the next. A hit
# ends at the next seek, or at a pause of 512 samples (11.6 ms) or more; its length is what was
# played, and its rate is `tickRate * length / duration` over that run.
#
# Eight hits here, matching the sample set the reference implementation (`vgm2fur`) writes into a
# Furnace module, name for name and rate for rate:
#
#   00 0x000000 5398 @ 14 783    04 0x0032D8  116 @  8 018
#   01 0x000000 1584 @  7 978    05 0x000000   96 @  8 301
#   02 0x001516 3810 @ 23 105    06 0x000060 1488 @  7 981
#   03 0x0023F8 3808 @ 23 128    07 0x000000   93 @  8 285
#
# 00 and 01 are one entry of the driver's table played for two different lengths, 05 and 07 are the
# 96- and 93-byte stop tones - the reference exports them too, so this does.

size 900 700

load "test_file/sega_md/02 Green Hill Zone.vgz"
tick

samples 8
sample-names ym2612dac_00_000000_01516
