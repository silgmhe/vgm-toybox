# The YM2612's DAC channel reaches the sample export.
#
# This chip is the odd one out: the others name a range of a ROM with registers, while the YM2612 is
# handed one byte at a time at 0x2A and the driver decides where those bytes came from. `02 Green
# Hill Zone` carries them in a `0x67` block of type 0x00 - 13 132 bytes - streamed into the DAC with
# `0x80-0x8F` and sought into with `0xE0`.
#
# **The unit is the region an `0xE0` seeks to.** The bank is not one sample: the driver's seek
# offsets are its sample table, and each entry is written at one fixed rate. Measured on this song,
# a visit to offset 0x1516 averages 1.90 ticks and to offset 0x32D8 5.47 - and neither varies by
# more than 0.02 ticks between visits. A single rate for the song matched half of it at best.
#
# Five regions here: 0x000000 (8.0 kHz), 0x000060 (8.0 kHz), 0x001516 (23.2 kHz), 0x0023F8
# (23.2 kHz), 0x0032D8 (8.1 kHz). The rate is the mean of the intervals in that region, because the
# intervals are dithered - 5.5 ticks arrives as 5 and 6 - and a median would quantise it to 8 820.
