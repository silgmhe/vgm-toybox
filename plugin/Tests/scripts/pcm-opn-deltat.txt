# Delta-T (OPN ADPCM-B) reaches the sample export.
#
# The OPN's two sample families are two blocks and two address page sizes: `0x82` with 256-byte pages
# for ADPCM-A, `0x83` (OPNB) or `0x81` (OPNA) with 8-bit pages for Delta-T. The bug this guards
# against is the one that made the root correction useless: a builder that answers one block for the
# whole family. Here the question is whether the *export* sees the second family at all, at the right
# address and the reference rate.
#
# `04 Stage 1` is an OPNB song; its Delta-T block sits at 0x83 and its first recording starts at
# 0x67B800, 0x5200 bytes long. The rate is 13 888.9 Hz - DELTA-N 0x4000 at 8 MHz - because a file is
# one recording, and the song's own DELTA-N is a transposition on top of that.

size 900 700

load "test_file/neo_geo/04 Stage 1.vgz"
tick

samples 115
sample-names opndeltat_67B800_05200
